## 1. Introduction

TIPS-G Alwar ("we", "our", or "the Institution") operates the **TIPS-G Student Attendance AI System** — an AI-powered, face-recognition-based attendance management platform ("the System"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share personal and biometric data from students, faculty, parents, and administrators who interact with the System.

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and operating in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including but not limited to the **Information Technology Act, 2000** and the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)** of India.

By enrolling in, operating, or otherwise using the System, you acknowledge and accept the practices described in this policy.

## 3. What Data We Collect

### 3.1 Student Personal Information
- Full name, roll number, and class/section/semester
- Contact details (phone number, email address)
- Parent/guardian name and contact information
- Document records stored in the `storage/documents` directory

### 3.2 Biometric Data *(Sensitive)*
- **Facial photographs** captured during the enrollment process
- **Facial embeddings** — numerical vector representations generated by the ArcFace AI model — derived from enrolled photographs and used exclusively for identity verification during attendance marking
- **Liveness signals** — blink patterns (Eye Aspect Ratio / EAR) and head-movement angles (yaw, pitch) captured transiently during attendance scans to prevent spoofing; these are **not stored** after verification

> **Important:** Facial embeddings are not raw photographs. They are mathematical vectors that cannot be reversed into the original image. However, they are still classified as biometric data under applicable law.

### 3.3 Attendance Records
- Date and time of every check-in event
- Attendance status: Present, Late, or Absent
- System confidence score associated with each recognition event

### 3.4 Notification & Communication Data
- Email addresses used for absence alerts
- WhatsApp/SMS numbers used for parent notifications (if enabled by the institution)

### 3.5 Network & Device Data
- Wi-Fi SSID/BSSID used for location validation to ensure attendance is marked on campus
- Application logs (stored locally in `logs/attendance_system.log`), which may include IP addresses and session identifiers

### 3.6 Authentication Data
- Staff/administrator usernames and hashed passwords
- JWT (JSON Web Token) session tokens — stored transiently and expire after 24 hours

## 5. Biometric Data — Special Provisions

Given the sensitive nature of facial recognition data, we apply the following additional safeguards:

1. **Explicit Consent:** Biometric enrollment of any student requires explicit written or digital consent from the student or, for minors, from a parent or legal guardian.
2. **Purpose Limitation:** Facial embeddings are used **only** for automated attendance marking within this System. They are never shared with third parties for commercial, marketing, or any other purpose.
3. **No Cross-Matching:** Student facial data will not be matched against any external face database, law enforcement system, or third-party platform.
4. **Liveness Data Transience:** Blink and head-movement data captured during liveness checks are processed **in real time in memory only** and are never written to disk or transmitted.
5. **Deletion on Request:** Biometric data (photos and embeddings) will be permanently deleted within **30 days** of a verified deletion request or upon the student's departure from the institution.
6. **Model Used:** Face recognition is performed using the **ArcFace** model with a **RetinaFace** detector, running locally on institution-controlled hardware or a secured VPS.

## 8. Data Security

We implement the following technical and organizational security measures:

- **Encryption in Transit:** All communication between the frontend application and backend API is conducted over HTTPS/TLS.
- **Encryption at Rest:** Data stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL and object storage) is encrypted at rest by the cloud provider.
- **Authentication:** Role-based access is enforced via JWT tokens signed with a secret key. Tokens expire automatically after 24 hours.
- **Wi-Fi Validation:** The System validates that attendance is marked only from the authorized campus Wi-Fi network (SSID: `TIPS-G-NETWORK`) to prevent remote spoofing.
- **Liveness Detection:** Anti-spoofing checks using blink detection (EAR threshold) and head-movement tracking prevent the use of photographs or video recordings to fake attendance.
- **Access Control:** Only authorized administrators and faculty members can access student records. Access is logged for auditing.
- **Local Deployment:** The AI inference pipeline (face detection and recognition) runs on institution-controlled infrastructure; facial images are processed locally and are not sent to external AI services.

Despite these measures, no system is completely immune to security threats. We will notify affected individuals of any confirmed data breach within the timeframes required by applicable law.
